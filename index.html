Project Topic

Data Science & Business Analytics Trends in 2026

Project Overview

This project explores what skills and roles are most in demand for data science and business analytics careers. The goal is to understand how AI, data tools, and changing hiring trends affect students who are preparing for analytics-related jobs.

Research Rationale

This topic is important because companies increasingly rely on data-driven decision making. Business analytics students need to understand which skills, tools, and job titles are most valuable in the current labor market. By studying job market trends, students can better prepare for future careers and make stronger decisions about skill development.

Brief Literature Review

Recent labor market research shows that AI and automation are changing the skills employers expect from job seekers. Technical skills such as Python, SQL, machine learning, and data visualization are becoming more important across industries. At the same time, communication, business understanding, and problem-solving skills remain essential for analytics professionals.
